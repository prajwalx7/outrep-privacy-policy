Account Deletion Instructions

Step-by-step instructions on how to permanently erase your OUTREP account and all associated workout, match, and profile records.

⚠️

Permanent Action Notice

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. Once deleted, all workout repetitions, rank tiers, experience points (XP), boss battle victories, match history, and friend connections are irreversibly purged.

📱 Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Recommended)

The fastest and most direct way to delete your account is within the OUTREP mobile application:

1

Open Profile Tab

Launch OUTREP on your mobile device and tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.

App > Profile
2

Access Account Settings

Scroll down and tap Manage Account (or Account Settings) located in the profile menu.

Profile > Manage Account
3

Confirm Deletion

Tap the red Delete Account option, review the consequences, and confirm to permanently wipe your profile and sign out.

Manage > Delete Account

📋 What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), here is the transparent breakdown of what happens when you delete your account:

Data Type Status After Request Action Taken
Profile & Credentials Permanently Deleted Display name, email address, avatar photo, username, and Firebase Authentication UID are permanently destroyed.
Workout Metrics & Reps Permanently Deleted All recorded rep counts (pushups, squats, lunges), exercise duration timestamps, and personal best records are erased.
XP, Levels & Streaks Permanently Deleted Level progress, XP transaction logs, streak records, and boss challenge history are purged immediately.
Matches & Challenges Permanently Deleted All 1v1 match participation records, battle outcomes, win/loss stats, and pending challenges are removed.
Social & Friend Relations Permanently Deleted Sent and received friend requests and friend list associations are severed immediately.

* Deletion occurs immediately upon confirmation, and your active session is signed out automatically.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account after deleting it?
No. Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, your XP, workout levels, wins, and friend connections cannot be restored. If you wish to use OUTREP again in the future, you will need to create a new account from scratch.
What happens to active subscriptions?
Deleting your OUTREP account does not automatically cancel subscriptions managed via the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To avoid unwanted renewal charges, please cancel any active subscriptions directly through your device's App Store or Google Play account subscriptions settings.
Are live video workouts stored or archived after account deletion?
No. OUTREP never records or stores live WebRTC workout video or audio streams on its servers at any time, even during normal active gameplay.
