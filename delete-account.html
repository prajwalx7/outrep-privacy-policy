How to Delete Your Account

Complete transparency on how to permanently delete your OUTREP: Compete With Friends profile, match records, and personal fitness metrics.

⚠️

Permanent Action Notice

Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. Once confirmed, all your pushup statistics, XP levels, match battle histories, friend connections, and boss raid accomplishments will be permanently erased from our servers.

📱 In-App Deletion Steps (Recommended)

You can instantly delete your account directly from within the OUTREP mobile application by following these 3 simple steps:

1

Open Profile Screen

Launch the OUTREP app on your device and tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

App > Profile Tab
2

Go to Manage Account

Under the Account section of your profile, tap on the Manage Account option.

Profile > Manage Account
3

Tap "DELETE ACCOUNT"

Scroll to the bottom of the Manage Account screen and tap the red DELETE ACCOUNT button. Confirm the dialog prompt to finish.

Manage Account > Delete Account > Confirm

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When you confirm account deletion in OUTREP: Compete With Friends, our backend immediately initiates the permanent deletion protocol:

Data Type Status After Deletion Details
User Profile & Identity Permanently Deleted Email address, username, display name, user ID, country code, and avatar photo URLs are completely deleted.
Workout & Repetition Logs Permanently Deleted All recorded pushup reps, squat reps, exercise durations, and exercise validation history are removed.
Progression & XP History Permanently Deleted Level progress, XP transaction logs, streak records, and boss challenge history are purged.
Matches & Challenges Permanently Deleted All 1v1 match participation records, battle outcomes, win/loss stats, and pending challenges are removed.
Social & Friend Relations Permanently Deleted Sent and received friend requests and friend list associations are severed immediately.

* Deletion occurs immediately upon confirmation, and your active session is signed out automatically.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account after deleting it?
No. Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, your XP, workout levels, wins, and friend connections cannot be restored. If you wish to use OUTREP again in the future, you will need to create a new account from scratch.
What happens to active subscriptions?
Deleting your OUTREP account does not automatically cancel subscriptions managed via the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To avoid unwanted renewal charges, please cancel any active subscriptions directly through your device's App Store or Google Play account subscriptions settings.
Are live video workouts stored or archived after account deletion?
No. OUTREP never records or stores live WebRTC workout video or audio streams on its servers at any time, even during normal active gameplay.
