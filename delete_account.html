Account Deletion Instructions

Step-by-step instructions on permanently deleting your OUTREP account, removing workout logs, XP, and profile details.

⚠️ Immediate and Permanent Action

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your workout statistics, match history, levels, XP, and friend links will be completely and irreversibly removed.

📱 Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Recommended)

Follow these steps inside the OUTREP mobile application:

1
Open Profile Screen
Launch OUTREP and tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
App > Profile
2
Tap Manage Account
Under the ACCOUNT section, tap Manage Account.
Profile > Manage Account
3
Tap Delete Account
Scroll to the bottom, tap the red DELETE ACCOUNT button, and confirm the prompt.
Manage > DELETE ACCOUNT

📋 Data Deletion Breakdown

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and data protection regulations:

Data Type Status Action Taken
Profile & Account Permanently Deleted Username, display name, email, avatar image, and Firebase Auth UID are permanently erased.
Workout Metrics & Reps Permanently Deleted All recorded pushup counts, workout timestamps, and personal records are purged immediately.
XP, Levels & Streaks Permanently Deleted Athlete levels, XP transaction logs, streak records, and boss raid history are removed.
Matches & Social Permanently Deleted 1v1 match results, friend lists, pending challenge requests, and head-to-head records are severed.
