04 Camera Usage & AI Repetition Detection

OUTREP utilizes your device camera combined with on-device computer vision algorithms (Google ML Kit Pose Detection) to analyze skeletal landmarks, posture angles, and repetition depth for pushups, squats, and associated exercises.

Solo camera detection occurs 100% in your device's local memory (RAM). Camera video frames for solo exercise detection are processed locally and never recorded, stored, or uploaded to our servers.
